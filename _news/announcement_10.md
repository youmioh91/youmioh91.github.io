🌱🌎 Our global soil sink paper was featured in **Eos**!

The Eos Research Spotlight, “**It Takes Three to Model Methane Right**,” highlights our recently published paper in *JGR: Biogeosciences*.

By combining process-based modeling, machine learning, and atmospheric inversions, we found that the global soil methane sink may be larger than previously estimated—around 40–45 Tg CH₄ yr⁻¹. This stronger soil sink also helps better reproduce atmospheric methane and its carbon isotope composition.

Many thanks to Eos for highlighting our work and to all of our co-authors and collaborators!
